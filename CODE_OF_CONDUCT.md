# Code of conduct

## Our pledge

We want Proofcast to be a welcoming, harassment-free project for everyone, regardless of age,
body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and
expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal
appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Our standards

Examples of behavior that helps:

- Being kind, patient and respectful, including when you disagree.
- Giving and gracefully accepting constructive feedback.
- Owning mistakes and learning from them.
- Focusing on what is best for the project and its users.

Examples of unacceptable behavior:

- Harassment, insults, or personal or political attacks.
- Sexualized language or imagery, or unwelcome advances.
- Publishing others' private information without their explicit permission.
- Promoting financial schemes, "signals" or pump-and-dump content in project spaces.
- Other conduct that would reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Scope

This code applies in all project spaces (issues, pull requests, discussions, and any other
channel run by the project) and when someone officially represents the project elsewhere.
